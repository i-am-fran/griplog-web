Training

Group your reps into sets

Structure a full training session, not just one block of reps. Turn on Sets for a finite-repeat timer and DuoTimer automatically inserts a longer rest between each set — no more restarting the timer or eyeballing your own break. A "Set X of Y" counter runs alongside your rep counter so you always know where you are.

Finite or endless

Full-screen, full focus

A black screen, a large countdown, and a color-coded progress ring. Nothing else. Each phase has its own color so you always know where you are at a glance.
