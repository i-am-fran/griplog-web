Two phases.

A minimalist interval timer for hangboard and grip training. Set work and rest independently. Run your protocol. Don't think about anything else.

Core

Two phases, timed independently

Set a work duration and a rest duration. They run back to back, cycling through your repeat count. Perfect for 7-second hangs with 3-second rests, or any protocol your training demands.

Repeats

Finite or endless

Set 1–99 repeats for a structured protocol, or enable infinite loop mode for open-ended sessions. A repeat counter keeps track during your workout.

  • 1–99 repeat count
  • Infinite loop mode
  • Repeat counter shown during run
Audio

Custom tones per phase

Choose a different sound cue for each phase. Know instantly whether you're starting work or rest without looking at the screen.

  • Independent tone per phase
  • Plays through silent mode
  • Distinct cue on phase transition
Running View

Full-screen, full focus

A black screen, a large countdown, and a color-coded progress ring. Nothing else. Each phase has its own color so you always know where you are at a glance.

  • Color-coded progress ring
  • Large, legible countdown
  • Distraction-free black background
Sync & Organise

Your timers, everywhere

Add a title and notes to each timer. iCloud sync keeps your protocols up to date across all your devices automatically, with no account required.

  • Title and notes per timer
  • iCloud sync via CloudKit
  • No account required
